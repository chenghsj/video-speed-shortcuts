# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026

Video Speed Shortcuts is designed to control HTML5 video playback entirely within the browser. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how it is stored.

## Data collection and transmission

Video Speed Shortcuts does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information, browsing history, website content, authentication information, financial information, analytics, diagnostics, or usage data to the developer or any third party.

The extension does not use analytics services, advertising services, tracking technologies, remote code, or external application servers. All fonts and application assets are included in the extension package.

## Settings stored by the browser

The extension stores only the settings needed to provide its features:

- enabled or disabled state;
- playback speed range, step, hold speed, and hold delay;
- keyboard shortcuts;
- indicator visibility;
- interface language and theme; and
- hostnames the user explicitly adds to the site scope list.

These settings are stored through the browser's `storage.sync` API. If browser synchronization is enabled, the browser provider may synchronize the settings between browsers signed in to the same account. This synchronization is controlled by the browser and its provider and is subject to the provider's privacy policy. Video Speed Shortcuts does not operate or receive data from that synchronization service.

## Website access

The extension requests access to `<all_urls>` so its content script can detect and control standard HTML5 video elements on websites and within permitted frames. Website content and playback activity are processed in the browser only and are not recorded or transmitted by the extension.

## Data retention and deletion

Settings remain in browser-managed extension storage until the user changes them, clears the extension's data, or removes the extension. Depending on browser synchronization settings, the browser provider may retain a synchronized copy according to its own policies.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the relevant browser-store disclosures will be updated before the changed behavior is released. The effective date above will also be revised.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the issue tracker of the repository where Video Speed Shortcuts is distributed.
